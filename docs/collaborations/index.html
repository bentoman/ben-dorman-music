Working together

A song might eventually have my name attached to it as composer, but that’s rarely where it actually begins.

It begins in a director’s living room, or on a Zoom call, or with someone saying, “You’ve really got to use this sometime.” I write and record something that suggests what a song might be. Then I hand it over.

And other people make it something else.

Tell me how the story feels. Together, we’ll find how it sounds.

John Lenihan

Director · A Christmas Carol with Heart, Nagoya Players, 2010

In 2010, John told me his idea for Dickens’s story: not Victorian London, but Texas. He pulled out photographs he’d taken on a trip there and talked about towns that seemed to have had the life slowly drawn out of them.

That conversation—and those photographs—gave me somewhere to go. The result was “This Town.”

Jeff Fritch

Writer, director and choreographer · Nagoya Players Junior, 2022–2024

Jeff and I go back to tap-and-guitar performances in 2006. In 2021 he asked me to write music for a children’s show he was developing, The Land of Kindness. When my first demo of “Shiny Gold Button” didn’t work, I asked him to just sing me the rhythm. That rhythm is the song.

“Home Sweet Home” also began as his idea, for a different show.

“Ben, your music compositions and lyrics still come to mind till this day. Thank you for the opportunity to work together and create music.”

Shawn Mahler

Producer · Nagoya Players Junior, 2022–

Shawn has produced every Nagoya Players Junior show I’ve written for. It was on a Zoom call with him that I first heard the brief for The Cat Who Walked by Herself: one song about the cat, and another about the First Magic. I had no idea what the First Magic meant. But I liked the idea.

“For NPJ, the music has to do several things at once. It needs to serve the story, give the performers something exciting to work with, be accessible to children with different levels of English and musical experience, and give us opportunities for movement and choreography. You’ve always done an incredible job checking all the boxes.”

Kory Alexander Majansky

Writer, director and co-lyricist · Nagoya Players Junior, 2026–

Kory wrote and directed Emma Amazing & The Bucket Fillers for the 2026 Showcase. The concept for “Big Blue Bucket” was his, and we wrote its lyrics together. We’re writing together again for Sweet Dreams, Eugene.

Before that, he was onstage in Romeo and Juliet. Kory and the backing singers took the bare bones of “Romeo’s Lament” and turned them into something much larger.

How a project usually runs

We start with how the story feels: the colours, feelings and vibes you have in mind. I write and record demos, you tell me what works and what doesn’t, and we keep going until it sounds right. When the songs are settled, I can provide vocal guides for rehearsal, and for some shows I’ve also worked as music director.

“Ben Dorman is wonderful to work with in a creative project. He is talented, intuitive, open, and versatile. He is also organized and professional: delivering all you need on time from drafts to vocal guides.”

Ana Valdes Lim
