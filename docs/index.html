About

About Ben

Ben Dorman is an Australian composer and lyricist based in Japan and available for collaborations anywhere. He creates songs and soundscapes for the stage. He begins by asking collaborators about colours, feelings and vibes, then searches for the musical language that captures them.

His own relationship with musical theatre began at school, performing in Guys and Dolls, Annie Get Your Gun, and original productions with music and song. Those experiences shaped a deeply personal, performer-first approach to composition.

“I want my work to carry young performers somewhere—and for their families to share the joy of seeing that happen. I hope the experience stays with them far beyond the stage.”

— Ben Dorman
