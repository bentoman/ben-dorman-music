Two new shows in the works

Two new Nagoya Players Junior shows are in the works. Mega Team Attack, for the Elementary Troupe, is a lively fantasy world with the energy of a video game; Sweet Dreams, Eugene, for the Pre-Teen Troupe, is a comedy adventure about imagination, creativity and the power of working together.

I’ve been discussing the music and lyrics with the writers, Jeff Fritch (Elementary) and Kory Alexander Majansky (Pre-Teen), alongside producer Shawn Mahler.

Jeff and I have been collaborating on performance and music for more than 20 years. He has been involved in most NPJ productions, apart from the most recent one, when I worked closely with Kory.

Each show offers a new opportunity to expand my musical palette. I don’t play video games, so I had never really listened to the rich, dramatic music used in games such as World of Warcraft. I found it a powerful experience, and learned a great deal from it while developing one of the pieces for Mega Team Attack.

For one of the songs in Sweet Dreams, Eugene, the brief was comic dancing—“think Marx Brothers.” I went with a Charleston-style piece: again, nothing like I’d written before.

I enjoy piecing the music together with fellow collaborators, whose feedback and insights inspire work the performers will, I hope, enjoy performing. If they take home memories of the music and songs, I could not ask for anything more.
